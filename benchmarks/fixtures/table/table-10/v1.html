Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the next billing date.

What counts as a user?

Anyone with edit access to your workspace. Guests who can only view or comment are free on paid plans.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off Starter and Pro. Contact us with proof of status.

How does the free trial work?

Paid plans include a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked. No credit card required.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, plus invoicing and bank transfer on annual Business and Enterprise plans.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Oregon). Enterprise customers can pin data to a region.
