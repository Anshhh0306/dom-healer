How to choose a backpacking tent

Start with capacity and weight: solo hikers chasing big miles often go for a 1P or trekking-pole shelter under 2 lb, while a 2P with two doors and two vestibules is the sweet spot for most couples. Three-season tents handle spring through fall; choose a four-season tent for snow loading and exposed alpine camps.

Not sure? Read our tent buying guide or chat with a gear specialist at your local store.
